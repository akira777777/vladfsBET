# VladfsBET Compliance & Responsible Gaming Framework

## 1. Regulatory Sandbox Operational State
- VladfsBET operates in a demonstration sandbox. All deposits, wagers, and withdrawals utilize virtual demonstration credits.
- Real-money transactional processing is strictly disabled until the platform operator secures valid gambling licenses, payment processor agreements, and regulatory approvals within applicable jurisdictions.

---

## 2. Age Restrictions & Jurisdictional Gating
- Underage gambling is strictly prohibited.
- Registration age verification is strictly enforced during onboarding (minimum 18 years, or 21 years for jurisdictions like the US).
- Players under the legal threshold are rejected at registration with an explicit regulatory error.

---

## 3. Responsible Gaming Tools
- **Deposit Limits**: Daily, Weekly, and Monthly maximum limits.
- **Loss Limits & Wager Limits**: Configurable financial boundaries.
- **Session Duration Reality Checks**: In-game timer banners providing reality checks every 15 minutes.
- **Cooling-Off Periods**: Temporary account freezes (24 hours to 30 days) with immediate session revocation.
- **Self-Exclusion**: Comprehensive lockout periods (6 months, 1 year, or permanent). Self-exclusions cannot be reversed by players during the active exclusion duration.

---

## 4. Know Your Customer (KYC) Requirements
- **Proof of Identity (PoI)**: Passport, National Identity Card, Driver's License.
- **Proof of Address (PoA)**: Utility bill or bank statement issued within the last 90 days.
- **Verification Workflow**: Automated checksum generation, document status tracking (`PENDING` -> `APPROVED` / `REJECTED` / `REQUIRES_INFORMATION`), and compliance audit logging.
